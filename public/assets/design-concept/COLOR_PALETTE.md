## Usage ratios
Approximate composition, not a rigid per-component formula:
- 65% dark foundations.
- 25% energetic pink/cyan/violet.
- 10% white, gold, coral and high-contrast highlights.

## Contrast and restraint
- Never use muted slate for essential gameplay values.
- Pink and cyan should remain distinguishable without relying on hue alone; pair them with labels, shape, or position.
- Reserve gold for high-value states, not routine decoration.
- Avoid large-area saturated gradients behind dense text.
- Glow should be a subtle edge/halo, not a permanent blur around every component.
