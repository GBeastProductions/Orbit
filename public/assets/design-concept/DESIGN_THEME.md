# ORBIT — Visual Direction

## Core identity
**Underground Rhythm Academy**: Japanese electronic-music culture, late-90s arcade/disco energy, and contemporary rhythm-game clarity.

The interface should feel like a real shipped rhythm game: deliberate, tactile, fast to read, and expressive without looking like a generic sci-fi dashboard.

## Visual balance
- 65% deep academy: midnight indigo, charcoal, restrained violet, quiet surfaces.
- 25% rhythm arcade: electric pink, cyan, lavender, confident color blocking.
- 10% accents: warm gold, coral, soft white.

## Design rules
1. Gameplay readability always outranks decoration.
2. Give each screen one dominant focal point.
3. Use glow only to communicate emission, selection, timing, or state.
4. Prefer crisp 2D geometry, typography, and controlled gradients over fake 3D bevels.
5. Use Japanese typography as considered editorial detail, never as random decoration.
6. Motion should reinforce rhythm, focus, feedback, or navigation.
7. Keep layouts responsive and preserve safe areas on mobile.
8. Do not imitate osu! or any single reference. Learn from its clarity and community energy, then make ORBIT's own visual language.

## Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk or a similar geometric sans.
- Japanese companion: Zen Kaku Gothic New.
- Compact technical labels: IBM Plex Mono.
- Avoid using more than two type families on one screen.

## Screen personality
- Title: optical-disc identity is the hero; surrounding controls stay quiet.
- Song select: track artwork and title lead; advanced metadata is secondary.
- Gameplay: playfield first, score/combo second, progress and accuracy always legible.
- Pause/settings: clear grouped panels, consistent controls, no decorative telemetry.
